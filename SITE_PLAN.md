# AFTERGLOW — site plan

## Positioning

AFTERGLOW is a modern late-night listening bar and dance room. The audience is style-aware guests looking for a place with credible music, intimate energy, and a clear weekly program. The site should make the atmosphere immediate and help someone decide whether to visit within one minute.

## Primary journey

1. Feel the room through a full-screen photographic opening.
2. Scan this week's three events and choose a night.
3. Understand the venue through sound, drinks, and atmosphere.
4. See authentic crowd photography.
5. Join the guest list or find practical visit details.

## Page structure

1. **Hero:** venue name, mood statement, hours, and two clear actions.
2. **This week:** three editorial event rows with artist, genre, and time.
3. **The room:** a short venue story plus sound, pour, and energy pillars.
4. **Gallery:** supplied photography in an asymmetrical editorial layout.
5. **Visit:** location model, opening hours, house note, and guest-list action.
6. **Guest-list dialog:** a functional local prototype ready to connect to a booking backend.

## Visual system

- **Thesis:** an editorial nightlife poster brought to life.
- **Palette:** black, warm cream, saturated coral, and a sharp acid-lime accent.
- **Typography:** oversized compressed-feeling sans-serif headlines with restrained serif italic notes.
- **Photography:** close, candid, slightly blurred, and emotionally immediate.
- **Motion:** subtle scroll reveals, a slow hero parallax, a ticker, and tactile hover transitions. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- **Responsive behavior:** the hierarchy stays bold on mobile, the event rows simplify, the gallery becomes vertical, and navigation becomes a focused menu.

## Content still needed before launch

- Final venue name and logo.
- City, address, map destination, and contact details.
- Real event dates, artist names, times, and ticket or guest-list rules.
- Instagram or other social URLs.
- Booking destination: email service, reservation platform, or custom API.
- Confirmation that the supplied photographs are cleared for public use.

## Delivery path

The site remains on localhost during design review. Once content and behavior are approved, the repository can be pushed to GitHub and imported into Vercel. The project is intentionally dependency-free, so Vercel can serve it as a static site without a build step.
